# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Tencent Game Audit a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, appearance, background, experience level, nationality, language, or identity.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Accepting good-faith corrections and differing technical opinions.
- Focusing criticism on evidence, code, and ideas rather than people.
- Respecting privacy, responsible disclosure, and regional differences.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, doxxing, discriminatory language, deliberate exposure of private data, and publishing exploitable vulnerability details outside the responsible-disclosure process.

## Responsibilities and enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate them. Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact method listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not use public issues to expose personal information. Security vulnerabilities must be reported through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not as conduct reports.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
